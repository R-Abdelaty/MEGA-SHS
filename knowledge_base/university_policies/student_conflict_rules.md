# Student Conflict Rules

Policy status: CORE CONFLICT RULE CONFIGURED; detailed workload limits require
confirmation.

## Confirmed rules

- A student cohort cannot be assigned to overlapping sessions.
- A repair must preserve valid cohort/course assignments.
- Minimize affected students and preserve unaffected sessions.
- Scheduling on a student's day off is a last resort in cancellation
  compensation.

## Requires confirmation

- Maximum daily load, travel time, consecutive sessions, and rules for attending
  a different same-major group were not supplied. Do not invent them.
