# Instructor and TA Rules

Policy status: CORE CONFLICT RULE CONFIGURED; workload details require
confirmation.

## Confirmed rules

- An instructor or TA cannot be assigned to overlapping sessions.
- Availability must be checked before a repair or compensation is proposed.
- Preserve unaffected instructor assignments where possible.
- Minimize the number of instructors/TAs affected by a repair.

## Requires confirmation

- Maximum workload, consecutive-session, travel-time, substitution, and
  fairness limits were not supplied. Do not invent them.
