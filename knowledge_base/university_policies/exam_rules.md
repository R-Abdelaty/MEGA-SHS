# Exam and Quiz Rules

Policy status: CONFIGURED from the existing scheduler policy and deterministic
validator configuration.

## Confirmed rules

- Exams and quizzes have higher disruption priority than lectures, tutorials,
  and labs.
- Final exams may start only at **09:00** or **14:00**.
- Quizzes should preferably use the **1st** or **5th** period. This is currently
  a soft rule, so violations are warnings unless a confirmed custom policy makes
  it hard.
- Teaching sessions cover Weeks 1–12. Final-exam scheduling occurs after the
  teaching period; teaching sessions must not be treated as running during the
  configured finals blackout.
- Exam room capacity and computerized-exam requirements must be verified from
  authoritative room and equipment data.

## Requires confirmation

- No additional exam-spacing, same-day assessment, invigilation, or exception
  rules were supplied. Request confirmation when those decisions are required.
