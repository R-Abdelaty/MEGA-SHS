# Tutorial, Lab, and Cohort Rules

Policy status: CONFIGURED, with one fallback procedure requiring confirmation.

## Confirmed rules

- Every tutorial and lab belongs to exactly one major and one cohort group.
- Never merge, relocate, or distribute a tutorial or lab into a group from
  another major.
- Shared-lecture metadata cannot be used to justify shared tutorials or labs.
- Cohort assignments must not create overlapping student sessions.

## Requires confirmation

- Exact eligibility, capacity, attendance, and approval rules for a same-major
  group transfer were not supplied. Treat that fallback as unavailable until
  confirmed for the specific case; it cannot be used to bypass the rule against
  scheduling a group on its normal day off.
