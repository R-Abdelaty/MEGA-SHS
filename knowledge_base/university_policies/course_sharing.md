# Course and Lecture Sharing Policy

Policy status: CONFIGURED from the existing scheduler prompt.

## Confirmed rules

- Cross-major sharing is allowed for lectures only.
- A shared course does not authorize a shared tutorial or lab.
- Catalog fields such as `Shared Lecture` and `Majors Sharing Lecture` are
  lecture-only metadata and must not be applied to tutorials or labs.
- A shared lecture must still satisfy student, instructor, room, capacity,
  equipment, and timing constraints.

## Requires confirmation

- No additional limits on which majors may share a lecture were supplied.
  Retrieve the course catalog and request confirmation if the sharing metadata
  is missing or ambiguous.
