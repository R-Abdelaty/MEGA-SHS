# Equipment Rules

Policy status: HIGH-LEVEL RULE CONFIGURED; detailed compatibility data requires
confirmation.

## Confirmed rules

- Do not create overlapping assignments for a shared piece or pool of equipment.
- A lab, computerized exam, or other equipment-dependent session may only use a
  room whose available equipment satisfies the session's confirmed requirement.
- Retrieve the lab/equipment schedule whenever equipment affects the decision.
- If required equipment or availability cannot be verified, the candidate is
  not ready for approval.

## Requires confirmation

- The existing policy does not define course-to-equipment compatibility,
  quantities, maintenance status, or substitution rules. Do not invent them.
