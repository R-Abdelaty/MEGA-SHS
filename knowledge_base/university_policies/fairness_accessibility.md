# Fairness, Accessibility, and Sustainability

Policy status: PLACEHOLDERS REQUIRE CONFIRMATION.

## Confirmed high-level requirements

- Scheduling decisions must consider academic fairness, accessibility, and
  sustainability.
- A candidate cannot be described as satisfying one of these requirements
  unless the relevant data and policy were evaluated.

## Requires confirmation

- No concrete accessibility accommodations, fairness metrics, room features,
  travel constraints, or sustainability thresholds were supplied.
- Retrieve authorized policy data or request user confirmation before making a
  decision that depends on any of these details.
