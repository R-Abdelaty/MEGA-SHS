# Session Priority Policy

Policy status: CONFIGURED from the existing scheduler prompt.

## Confirmed rules

- During disruption resolution, use this priority order:
  **quiz/exam > lecture > tutorial > lab**.
- Priority does not authorize a conflict, an invalid assignment, or silent
  relaxation of another hard constraint.
- If similarly valid repairs remain after priorities and hard constraints are
  applied, present multiple options for approval.

## Requires confirmation

- Priority positions for workshops, presentations, faculty meetings, shared
  equipment, and other session types were not defined in the existing policy.
  Do not invent their relative order.
