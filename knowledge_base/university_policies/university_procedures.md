# GUC and GIU Scheduling Procedures

Policy status: PROJECT CONFIGURATION, with real-deployment confirmation required
for any policy not represented in authoritative university data.

## Confirmed project conventions

- The teaching week uses five periods:
  - P1: 08:30–10:00
  - P2: 10:15–11:45
  - P3: 12:00–13:30
  - P4: 13:45–15:15
  - P5: 15:45–17:15
- Breaks are 15 minutes after P1, P2, and P3, and 30 minutes after P4.
- The configured teaching semester contains 12 teaching weeks.
- Decisions may require the General Schedule, Room Schedule, Doctors Schedule,
  Exam Schedule, enrollment, equipment, or other supporting schedules.
- Policy and schedule facts must be retrieved from authoritative sources before
  they are used.

## Requires confirmation

- These are the current project conventions supplied during development. If an
  uploaded authoritative schedule or approved GUC/GIU policy differs, stop and
  obtain confirmation rather than silently choosing one source.
